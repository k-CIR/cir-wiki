title: Preparation

Preparation covers the participant-facing setup that must be completed before every MEG session at NatMEG. This includes attaching the required electrodes and recording the head shape and coil locations so the measurement can be monitored and analysed correctly.

This section describes the standard preparation procedure at NatMEG, including:

- [Applying electrodes](./01_Electrodes-standard-setup.md)
- [Digitization of head shape and HPI coils](./02_Digitization-hpi.md)
